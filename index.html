On-time Delivery & Margins

The threshold effect in service levels

A regression on 21,316 orders showing where service improvement actually pays off.

View analysis
On-time delivery vs. gross margin lift hover to inspect any point
Every 1-point improvement in on-time delivery was associated with a 0.4-point increase in gross margin, but only after service levels crossed 92%. Below that, discounting was eroding the gains.

Reading the regression

A metric is only useful if it as the capacity to change your strategy.

That threshold effect implies two distinct jobs.

Below 92%

Fix the floor

Stop spreading effort evenly. Consolidate volume, simplify routes, add buffer capacity to get accounts over 92% fastest.

  • Leave pricing alone here
  • A move from 89% to 90% is nearly worthless
  • Cost to cross 92% is an entry fee
Above 92%

Harvest the premium

Put capital into lanes already above the line and recover unclaimed margin.

  • Pull back rebates after 93‑94% held for a quarter
  • Reward managers for time above threshold
  • Don't average the whole network
The marginal‑ROI trap

Do not apply the 0.4 multiplier across the whole network. It holds only for volume shipped after an account crosses 92%.

Slotting is about how often items travel together.

Cluster 1

High‑frequency, lightweight

Put them at arm's reach in the golden zone.

  • Reduce walking up to 40%
  • Use gravity flow racks
  • Co‑locate by frequency
Cluster 2

Push to the periphery on lower shelves.

  • Protect pickers
  • Use pallet positions

The co‑picking trap

Slotting by category alone splits clusters that are picked together, creating unnecessary travel.

On AI

The promise, and the risk, of AI integration.

Used well, AI sharpens decisions. Used carelessly, it erodes them. We size AI to the business it serves; vendors size it to their sales targets.

Where it helps

AI can sharpen a decision

Done well, it shortens the distance between a question and a good answer: faster triage of exceptions, a stronger first draft, patterns a person might take weeks to find on their own.

  • Faster triage of anomalies & exceptions
  • Consistent first-pass analysis across teams
  • Shifts analysts' time from data wrangling to judgment calls
Where it hurts

It can just as easily erode one

We routinely see companies overspend on AI, or deploy it so it leaks sensitive data or answers the same question differently depending on who asks. Smaller organizations often carry more of this risk than they expect.

  • Inconsistent answers across functions or time periods
  • Sensitive data exposure through the wrong integration
  • Rising token costs against thinning business context
We help you implement AI with the right business, statistical, and data context: a right-sized approach that stays consistent across functions and over time, but flexible enough to grow as your needs change. Getting that balance wrong is a real shortcoming even at the largest companies. For smaller organizations the exposure is often greater still, particularly as tokens grow more expensive and well-specified business context stays scarce.
